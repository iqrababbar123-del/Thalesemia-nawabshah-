About Us

A beacon of hope for thalassaemic children across Sindh — providing free, safe and timely treatment since 2005.

Thalassaemia Patients Welfare Society Nawabshah is a non-profit welfare organization running Thalassaemia Care Centre Nawabshah. The centre is a hope for more than 2,600 registered thalassaemic children from 15 districts of Sindh.

Our mission is to provide 100% free, safe and timely blood transfusions, free medicines and laboratory facilities to poor children. We are working for a "Thalassaemia Free Pakistan" by promoting pre-marriage screening.

Our Mission

To provide free treatment and to make Pakistan Thalassaemia Free.

Funding Source: The centre runs purely on Zakat, Donations and support of philanthropists. There is no regular government budget.
